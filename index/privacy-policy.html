Privacy Policy

Privacy information for the DEVX LIMITED company website.

Last updated: 30 September 2026

1. Scope

This policy describes information handling for the DEVX LIMITED company website and related email enquiries. It does not automatically cover every mobile application developed by the company. Please read the app-specific privacy policy provided in the relevant app or store listing.

2. Information processed

When you email us, we receive your email address, your name if provided, your message and any attachments. Providing this information is voluntary, but missing details may prevent us from responding or assisting fully. Please do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary sensitive data. The website hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser types and request logs to operate and protect its service.

3. Purposes

Correspondence is used to respond to enquiries, discuss projects, provide support and manage business relationships. Technical data supports website operation and security. This website does not integrate advertising code or behavioral analytics.

4. Service providers

Email and hosting providers may process information needed to deliver their services, in Hong Kong or other locations where they operate. Information may also be disclosed when required by law. Please agree on appropriate channels and terms before sharing confidential project information.

5. Retention and security

Information should be kept only as long as needed to address an enquiry, carry out agreed work and meet relevant legal obligations. The specific period depends on the correspondence and service agreement. No electronic transmission or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your information requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you provided by contacting admin@devxlimited.com. Identity verification may be needed before a request is processed. Deletion may be limited by legal obligations or unresolved matters.

7. Cookies and external services

This website’s code does not set cookies, use localStorage or include advertising trackers. The hosting service may use cookies necessary for security or access controls. See our Cookie Policy. Email services and external sites are subject to their providers’ policies.

8. Updates and contact

This policy may change as the website or information handling changes. The revision date appears above. Contact DEVX LIMITED at admin@devxlimited.com or by post at Room 55, Flat A, 7/F, Wing Tai Centre, 12 Hing Yip Street, Kwun Tong, Hong Kong.
