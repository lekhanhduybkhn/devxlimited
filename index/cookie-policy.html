Cookie Policy

Information about cookies and browser storage.

Last updated: 30 September 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit a website. Some support essential functions, while others can be used for analytics or advertising.

2. What does this website use?

The DEVX LIMITED website code does not set cookies, store data in localStorage or integrate third-party advertising or analytics tools.

3. Hosting services

The hosting platform may use cookies or session mechanisms to protect its service or manage access, particularly for private previews. These mechanisms are operated by the hosting provider.

4. Your controls

You can manage or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may affect access managed by the hosting platform. For questions, contact admin@devxlimited.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
